# Privacy boundary

SentinelFlow ships with synthetic data only. A customer deployment requires a
documented controller/processor relationship, purpose limitation, lawful basis,
retention schedule, subject-rights process, cross-border transfer assessment,
security review, and deletion/export workflow appropriate to that customer and
jurisdiction. None is provided or certified by this repository.

Collect only tokenised identifiers and event attributes necessary for the agreed
risk purpose. Do not send raw PAN, CVV, passwords, authentication secrets, or
full identity documents. Case notes and evidence may contain personal data in a
real deployment, so they require access controls, retention, redaction, and audit
policies beyond the synthetic demo.
